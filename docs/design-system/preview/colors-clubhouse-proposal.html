Warm paper, not black

Default light theme is cream, not white. It softens the screen and signals "newsletter from the committee" instead of "live broadcast".

02

Oxblood, not signal red

Brand red dialled from #E10600 to #8A1F1A — still unmistakably F1, but reads as a club crest, not a hazard light.

03

Brass for stars

Stars are the prized metric. Brass (#B8893F) gives them a trophy feel — the same warmth you'd see engraved on a wooden honours board.

04

Sentence case, Danish first

Buttons and labels in sentence case. Copy written like Per is in the room, not like the chief race director is on the PA.
